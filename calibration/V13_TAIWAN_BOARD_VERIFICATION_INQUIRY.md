# Calibration v13 — Taiwan IRB/REC Verification Inquiry Pack

Status: **prepared — no board contact authorized**

This pack defines the exact questions that must be answered before a candidate review board may be
considered for selection.

It is not a contact authorization and does not send anything.

## Inquiry identity boundary

Do not send a verification inquiry until a private applicant-context record is complete enough to
accurately state whether the applicant is:

- institution-affiliated, or
- unaffiliated.

The inquiry should disclose only the minimum information needed to answer intake questions.

Do not attach participant data, assessment responses, IDs, or private research datasets.

## Standard verification questions

For each candidate board, obtain a documented answer to all of the following:

1. Is the board currently accepting **new** external submissions?
2. If the PI is unaffiliated, may an unaffiliated individual submit directly?
3. If not, what institutional sponsor, department, supervisor, or site authorization is required?
4. Will the board review a prospective online, nonclinical cognitive/psychometric human-subjects study?
5. Is public-web recruitment outside the hospital acceptable, and what study-site authorization is required?
6. Which review route should be requested for applicability determination or initial review?
7. What current new-application review fee applies?
8. What human-research ethics training hours/certificates are required for the PI and research staff?
9. Which forms and submission system are currently required?
10. Are there additional requirements for privacy, consent, withdrawal/deletion, or Taiwan-wide online recruitment?
11. If an applicability/exemption determination is requested before a full study submission, does the board provide that service?
12. What is the current intake/capacity status and any submission deadline?

## Evidence rule

A board-verification field may become `verified=true` only when backed by either:

- a current official public page/document; or
- a direct written reply from the board retained in a private, non-public record.

Phone-only information must be followed by written confirmation before it becomes frozen evidence.

## Response privacy

The public repository may store:

- board name/id;
- boolean or enumerated verification outcomes;
- official public URLs;
- verification date;
- a non-public record reference.

Do not commit:

- a private email thread;
- personal email addresses that are not already official public contacts;
- signatures;
- phone call notes containing personal information;
- applicant identity/contact details.

## Selection remains locked

A complete inquiry response does not automatically select a board.

Selection remains a separate governance step after:

- private applicant context is complete;
- required verification fields are evidenced;
- current qualification/capacity are rechecked;
- no unresolved mismatch exists.
