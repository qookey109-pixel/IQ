## No ranking

The repository must not score, rank, or automatically select a board.

Selection requires the real applicant context:

- PI legal name and role;
- institutional affiliation or unaffiliated route;
- official research/data-rights contacts;
- funding declaration;
- conflict-of-interest declaration.

Before selecting any board, separately confirm:

1. current qualified status;
2. external/unaffiliated intake acceptance;
3. online behavioral/psychometric scope;
4. submission process;
5. fees;
6. training and institutional-authorization requirements.
